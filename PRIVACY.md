# Privacy by Design

Vanta works with unusually sensitive personal behaviour. Privacy is a product feature, not a footer.

## Data-minimisation rules

1. Do not collect explicit sexual details merely because they are available.
2. Do not retain browsing history when a local category/allow-block decision is sufficient.
3. Do not record or transcribe ordinary Guide calls.
4. Do not make Guides write free-form sexual-history notes by default.
5. Do not expose the reason a member joined Vanta to reward partners.
6. Do not expose behavioural-private data to the community identity.
7. Do not use call content to train models.
8. Do not require a real public name.

## Identity separation

A member may have:
- account identity for login / payment;
- behavioural record visible only to authorised product flows;
- community alias;
- Guide-facing member code / alias.

## Guide call metadata

Permitted examples:
- scheduled time;
- connected / missed / cancelled;
- duration;
- language;
- next-call preference;
- structured safety / operational flag where necessary.

Not retained in ordinary operation:
- audio;
- transcript;
- AI-generated transcript summary;
- detailed free-form sexual disclosure.

## Partner rewards

A partner should receive only what is required to fulfil a reward, e.g. a redeemable token and reward SKU. They should not receive the member's PMO goal, browsing information, community profile or Guide-call details.

## Financial data

V1 should work with manual spending boundaries / logs. If bank or transaction connectors are introduced later, they must be opt-in, narrowly scoped and clearly explain what is imported.

## Community

Public/pseudonymous community content is a different privacy surface from private behavioural data. Never auto-post a user's logs, slips, blocked attempts, spending or Guide-call outcomes.

## Legal / policy work before launch

Before a production launch, obtain jurisdiction-specific review for:
- privacy policy;
- consent and data processing;
- age gating (V1 should be 18+);
- tele-support vendor contracts;
- safety escalation;
- app-store policy;
- financial-data integrations;
- health/wellbeing marketing claims;
- data retention and deletion rights.
