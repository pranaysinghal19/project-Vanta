# Challenges & Rewards

## Why challenges exist

Vanta should not leave a vacuum when a member reduces a habit. Challenges make positive replacement behaviour visible and rewarding.

## Example challenge catalogue

- Move 12 — 12 workouts / movement sessions in 30 days
- Sleep Reset — hit a member-selected sleep target 20 nights
- Read 300 — read 300 minutes in a month
- Focus 20 — complete 20 focused work/study blocks
- Outside 20 — complete 20 outdoor sessions
- Honest 14 — complete 14 honest daily check-ins
- Keep It — remain within a self-selected discretionary-spending boundary

## Rewards

Small partner benefits may include:
- fitness offers;
- sports / outdoor offers;
- books / learning;
- wellbeing services;
- activities;
- selected lifestyle partners.

The reward should reinforce the habit the member is building.

## Privacy

Reward partners receive no PMO or sexual-behaviour data.

They should only receive an eligibility/redemption token and the minimum fulfilment data required.

## Fraud resistance

Keep early rewards small enough that fraud is not economically attractive.

Verification tiers:
- trust-based;
- app/system verified;
- device-assisted where the user grants permission;
- partner verified where appropriate.

Do not turn Vanta into a surveillance system just to police a 10% discount.
