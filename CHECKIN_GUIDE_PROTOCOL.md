# Vanta Guide — Fortnightly Check-In Protocol

## Role

A Vanta Guide is a trained accountability/listening professional or contracted tele-support agent. The role is not automatically therapy, counselling, psychology, medicine or addiction treatment.

## Call target

Typical duration: **10–15 minutes**.

Frequency: **approximately every 14 days / two calls per month** for the Guided membership.

## Privacy promise

Ordinary calls are:
- not recorded;
- not transcribed;
- not listened to by an AI model;
- not converted into detailed free-form sexual-history notes.

The system may retain minimal call operations metadata.

## Recommended flow

### 1. Open humanly

> “How have the last two weeks actually been?”

### 2. Reality-check the logs

> “Do you feel what you've logged reflects how the fortnight really went?”

If no:

> “That's alright. You're not being graded. We'd rather have an honest picture than a perfect one.”

The member should correct private records themselves where possible.

### 3. Ask what helped

> “What seemed to make the good days easier?”

### 4. Ask what was difficult

> “When did sticking to your plan feel hardest?”

Keep conversation non-graphic.

### 5. Check the replacement life

> “You wanted to get back to the gym / improve sleep / save money. How did that go?”

### 6. Next commitment

> “What's one thing you want to make easier for yourself over the next two weeks?”

## Tone

- curious, not interrogating;
- warm, not sentimental;
- no moral judgement;
- no shaming;
- no false medical claims;
- no trying to “catch” the member lying;
- accuracy over performance.

## Safety

A production tele-support programme needs a written escalation path for immediate danger, abuse, self-harm disclosures or other critical scenarios. It must be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which Vanta operates.
