# Vanta Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation (this repository)

- locked brand mark: Core Flow / Concept 01;
- product doctrine;
- Goal Engine contracts;
- privacy/data separation;
- community rules;
- Guide protocol;
- challenge/reward model;
- initial Supabase schema;
- responsive web shell.

## Phase 1 — Member MVP

- authentication;
- onboarding;
- custom Reduce / Build goals;
- daily check-ins;
- manual paid-content spending boundary/logging;
- replacement habits;
- monthly review;
- pseudonymous community;
- challenge catalogue;
- reward unlock tokens;
- Guide scheduling;
- minimal Guide operations dashboard.

## Phase 2 — Protection

- Android protection adapter;
- browser extension;
- Decision Lock server authority;
- emergency 72-hour protection mode;
- cross-device rule sync;
- platform capability matrix.

## Phase 3 — Wider platform coverage

- iOS capability-compliant protection;
- Windows/macOS desktop protection;
- deeper device health/activity integrations with explicit consent;
- optional spending-data integrations.

## Phase 4 — Scale

- tele-support QA without recording content;
- guide capacity / routing;
- multilingual operations;
- partner reward marketplace;
- experimentation and retention analytics;
- additional private-habit programmes where appropriate.

## Product success criteria

Vanta should be judged by whether members become more honest, more consistent and more engaged with the life they are building — not merely whether a streak counter becomes large.
