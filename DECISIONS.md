# Locked Decisions

This file exists so future agents, designers and contractors do not casually undo product decisions already made.

## Brand

### LOCKED — name
**Vanta** is the working product / company name.

> Note: legal, trademark and domain clearance is still required before public launch. Do not rename the product in code or design without an explicit owner decision.

### LOCKED — primary app mark
**Concept 01 — Core Flow** is the primary logo/app icon direction.

Canonical idea:
- rounded-square app icon;
- near-black background;
- single flowing ribbon / S-curve form;
- coral → pink/lilac → violet gradient;
- subtle depth at the fold;
- no literal flame;
- no shield, mountain, arrow, animal, warrior, dumbbell or “alpha” motif;
- must remain legible at small app-icon / favicon size.

Primary source asset: `assets/brand/vanta-flow-primary.svg`.

### LOCKED — emotional brand direction
Vanta should feel:
- alive;
- optimistic;
- forward-moving;
- human;
- discreet;
- premium but not luxury-cosplay;
- youthful without looking childish.

Vanta should **not** feel:
- gym-bro / bodybuilding;
- “alpha male”;
- yoga / meditation;
- home décor / spa;
- corporate compliance software;
- cybersecurity;
- crypto;
- self-help guru;
- clinical addiction treatment.

### PROVISIONAL — copy
Current working lines may change after user testing:
- `Less hiding. More living.`
- `Same you. A better next.`
- `Choose what goes. Choose what replaces it.`

Do not treat provisional lines as immutable brand law.

## Product

### LOCKED — membership model
Vanta is a **monthly accountability membership**, not merely a blocker subscription.

### LOCKED — human calls
Guided membership includes **two private human accountability check-ins per month** (roughly fortnightly), provided by trained Vanta Guides or a contracted tele-support partner.

Ordinary Guide calls:
- are not therapy;
- are not recorded;
- are not transcribed;
- are not analysed by an AI listener;
- retain only minimal operational metadata required to run the service.

### LOCKED — community direction
The community is for men improving their lives, not for sexual discussion.

Allowed themes include recovery, discipline, sleep, fitness, focus, study, career, money, friendships, loneliness, confidence, stress, routines and progress.

Disallowed culture includes explicit sexual detail, porn content, hookup/conquest bragging, sexual solicitation, objectification and misogyny.

### LOCKED — member-defined goals
Vanta does not prescribe one sexual philosophy. A member can choose:
- no pornography;
- no PMO;
- reduce pornography;
- reduce masturbation;
- time-based boundaries;
- paid adult-content spending limits;
- or custom goals.

Positive replacement goals are first-class product objects.

### LOCKED — cross-platform principle
One Vanta account carries the member's goals across platforms, while each device can have its own enforcement rules.
