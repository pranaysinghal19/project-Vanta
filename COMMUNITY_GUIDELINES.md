# Vanta Community Guidelines

## Purpose

Vanta Community is a pseudonymous space for men to improve their lives together.

The community is not a porn forum, dating service, hookup forum, explicit confession board, or “alpha male” status contest.

## The simple rule

> **Discuss the problem, not the pornography.**

## Good topics

- urges and triggers without graphic detail;
- routines that help;
- sleep;
- fitness;
- walking / outdoors;
- study and career;
- reading;
- spending and saving;
- loneliness and friendships;
- confidence and communication;
- relationships in non-graphic terms;
- setbacks and resets;
- challenge progress;
- wins;
- asking for support.

## Not allowed

- pornographic or sexually explicit content;
- links/names designed to help others find porn;
- graphic sexual storytelling;
- hookup / sexual-conquest bragging;
- solicitation or sexual DMs;
- sexualised images;
- fetish discussion intended to arouse;
- misogyny or degrading women;
- harassment;
- shaming members for slips;
- medical misinformation stated as fact;
- pressuring someone to adopt a sexual value they did not choose;
- “semen retention gives superpowers” style claims presented as science.

## Recovery culture

A member does not lose social standing because of one slip. Helpful participation, consistency, honesty and support for others should matter more than streak length.

## Moderation

Moderation tools should support:
- report;
- block;
- content removal;
- warnings;
- temporary restrictions;
- community suspension;
- permanent bans for serious/repeated abuse;
- separate voice/check-in restrictions where necessary.
